Choosing a plumber in Ottawa

A good first call should leave you with a clearer idea of what happens next. Tell the plumber whether the issue is in a home, rental property, or business, and describe what you can see. A plumber in Ottawa may be called for an active leak, a blocked drain, a sewer line backup, frozen pipes, or plumbing planned as part of a renovation. The right next step depends on the specific problem, so avoid settling on a cause before it has been checked.

When you compare options, ask who the work is for, what information the plumber needs, and how they’ll explain the recommended work. If water is actively escaping or a sewer backup is underway, call promptly instead of waiting for a convenient time.

What to have ready before the visit

A little preparation helps the plumber understand the situation and makes it easier to discuss the work. If possible, have these details ready:

  • The property address and whether it is a home, rental, or business.
  • A plain description of what is happening and when you first noticed it.
  • Any areas affected, such as a particular drain or part of the property.
  • Recent plumbing changes or renovation work that may be relevant.
  • Your main concern, such as stopping a leak or planning work before renovation.

Keep the work area accessible if it is safe, but don’t take plumbing apart to investigate. Avoid putting off an active leak or sewer backup, and don’t assume a recurring issue will resolve without attention. Sharing what you know is more helpful than trying a risky fix first.
