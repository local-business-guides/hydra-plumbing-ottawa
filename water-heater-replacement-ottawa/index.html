Get ready for the assessment

A little preparation can help the visit go smoothly and make it easier to explain the problem. Before calling, jot down:

  • Whether you’ve seen a leak, reduced hot water, or temperature changes.
  • When the issue first appeared and whether it comes and goes.
  • The heater’s location and how a plumber can reach it.
  • Any planned renovations or changes in how the property is used.
  • Questions you have about the proposed work and what happens next.

Don’t try to diagnose the equipment from one symptom alone. Avoid taking apart plumbing connections or ordering a new unit before a plumber has checked what the property needs. If the heater is in a difficult-to-access area, explain that when you arrange the visit. For a larger renovation, ask whether City of Ottawa permits apply to the plumbing work, especially if the property is older or heritage-listed.
